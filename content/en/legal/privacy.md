## What this site collects

Very little. There are no accounts, no tracking pixels and no advertising.

## Analytics

Page views and referrers are counted with cookieless analytics. No personal data and no cookies are involved, and nothing is used to follow you across other sites.

## Contact form

When you use the contact form, what you enter is sent through Web3Forms to hello@victorgarces.me: your name, your email, the topic you choose, your message and, depending on the topic, the timeline and budget range or the date and place. It is used only to reply to you.

## Hosting and media

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Images and video are served from this site and, once the media pipeline is live, from media.victorgarces.me on AWS CloudFront.

## Embeds

There are no YouTube or Instagram embeds. Links to those platforms open them in a new page, under their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy or the deletion of anything you sent through the form by writing to hello@victorgarces.me.
