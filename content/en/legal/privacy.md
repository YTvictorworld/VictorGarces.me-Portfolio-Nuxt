## What this site collects

Very little. There are no accounts, no tracking pixels and no advertising.

## Analytics

There is no analytics on this site today: nothing counts your visit. If that changes, this page will say which tool is used and what it measures, and it will stay cookieless.

## Contact form

When you use the contact form, what you enter is sent to hello@victorgarces.net: your name, your email, the topic you choose, your message and, depending on the topic, the timeline or the date and place. It is used only to reply to you.

The form is handled by [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/privacy), a third-party service. Your browser sends the message to it directly, so it sees your IP address, and it stores the submission before forwarding it to me. If no form service is configured, the button opens your own email app instead and nothing passes through a third party.

## On your device

One entry in local storage remembers whether you chose the Developer or the Filmmaker side, one cookie remembers your language, and some pages keep a cached copy of this site's public text so they load faster. None of it identifies you. The full list is on the [cookies page](/cookies).

## Hosting and media

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Images and video are served from this site.

## Embeds

There are no YouTube or Instagram embeds. Links to those platforms open them in a new page, under their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy or the deletion of anything you sent through the form by writing to hello@victorgarces.net.
