I make short films and photograph events in Santo Domingo. I write, direct and edit, usually with a small crew and one camera.

What I care about is storytelling: composition, lighting and the cut that makes a moment land. ¿Por qué no?, my 2026 short, is one actress and two inner voices in a room lit in red and blue.

Since 2025 I also cover conferences, launches and community events as a photographer.
