I am Víctor Garcés, a developer from Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, and a Software Engineering student at UNAPEC. I started with a technical degree in computer applications and spent more than three years building modern, responsive front-ends.

Today I work across the whole stack: Vue and Nuxt on the front, C#/.NET, SQL databases, Docker, microservices and AWS on the back, which I have been doing professionally for almost a year.

I am also a filmmaker, photographer and video editor, so I care about composition, light and rhythm as much as clean code. I bring both to the work: interfaces that are functional and good to look at.
